## What it is:
A web application where Rajarata University students can upload, search, and download study materials (notes, past papers, study guides) instead of relying on scattered WhatsApp groups and shared drives.

## Why it's needed:
Students currently struggle to find reliable study material because it's spread across informal channels. This project centralizes everything into one searchable, organized platform.

## Who it's for:
Mainly first-year and second-year Rajarata University students
Also senior students (sharing notes), lecturers (pointing to resources), and students from other faculties

## Core Features:
:-Upload & Manage Resources – Users register/login and upload PDFs tagged by subject, category, semester
:-Search & Filter – JavaScript-powered instant search by keyword, subject, category, or file type
:-Ratings & Comments – Users rate and comment on resources to highlight the most useful ones
:-User Authentication & Dashboard – Secure login with hashed passwords, personal dashboard for uploads/saved items
:-Contact/Feedback Form – Students can report issues or request resources
